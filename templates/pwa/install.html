Install {{ app_name }}

Add {{ app_name }} to your home screen or desktop to open it like an app. It is the same website: you sign in the same way, and it needs an internet connection for every page. Nothing is saved on your device for offline use.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the browser menu (three dots).
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The {{ app_name }} icon appears with your other apps.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this site in Safari. Other iPhone browsers may not offer this.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu.
  2. Choose Install {{ app_name }} (Chrome) or Apps → Install this site as an app (Edge).

To remove it, uninstall it like any other app: press and hold the icon on a phone, or use the app's menu on a computer.
