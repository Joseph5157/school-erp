# Product

## Product vision

FROZEN: Build a reliable and maintainable School ERP for the daily operation of a school.

Priorities:
- clear workflows over feature quantity
- reliable data over clever automation
- simple infrastructure over distributed architecture
- long-term maintainability
- real school workflows rather than generic CRUD
- ability to understand and maintain the system even after long periods without active development

Initial product is for one school. Multi-school SaaS is not part of the initial product.

## Primary users

### School Administrator

Responsibilities:
- school setup
- academic setup
- admissions
- students
- classes/sections
- fees
- reports
- operational administration

### Teacher

Responsibilities:
- daily teacher workflows
- assigned classes
- students
- attendance
- assessments
- relevant student information
- announcements

### Parent/Guardian

Responsibilities:
- child-specific information
- attendance
- fees
- results
- announcements
- appropriate communication

### Student

Responsibilities:
- student-appropriate academic information
- profile
- attendance
- timetable later
- assessments/results later
- announcements later

FROZEN: Do not expose the admin ERP directly as teacher, parent, or student portals.

## Product principles

FROZEN:
- The system should prioritize dependable operational workflows over broad feature count.
- School records must remain understandable, correct, and historically accurate.
- The product must serve the daily needs of a single school before considering scale expansion.
- Maintainability and clarity matter more than short-term sophistication.

CURRENT:
- Phase 1 focuses on the student lifecycle foundation and core administrative capabilities.
- Communication and reporting will grow later based on clear requirements rather than assumptions.
